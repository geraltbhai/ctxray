Questions

What happens when I cancel?

Your key works until the end of the period you paid for, plus five days. After that the Pro commands stop and the free scanner keeps working exactly as before. Nothing is deleted and nothing phones home.

Does it send my code anywhere?

No. ctxray makes no network calls, free or Pro. The licence check is an offline signature check on your machine.

How accurate is the token count?

It uses a built-in estimator fitted against a real tokenizer, with about 6% median error. That is enough to rank files and price a repo; the README shows the calibration method.

Can the licence check be bypassed?

Yes, trivially, and the source says so. It exists to make paying the obvious path for people who find the tool useful, not to fight anyone.
